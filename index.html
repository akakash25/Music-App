About Azure

Azure is a minimal music player designed to offer a distraction-free audio experience. Featuring 15 curated ambient tracks, dynamic mood-based theming, and seamless playback control, Azure is your personal companion for focus sessions, deep work, and relaxation.

Built with modern web technologies and loaded directly from GitHub for fast access, Azure ensures your favorite music is always just a click away.
